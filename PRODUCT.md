# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers coordinating multiple OpenCode agents across projects and teams. They use the dashboard during active work to identify exceptions, locate the responsible agent or task, inspect recent context, and decide what needs attention next.

## Product Purpose

Provide a live operational view of Ensemble teams that makes parallel work easy to triage. Success means errors, blocked tasks, stalled agents, and important messages are discoverable without horizontal scrolling or guesswork, and each signal leads directly to its underlying context.

## Brand Personality

Focused, operational, precise. The interface should feel calm under pressure, technically credible, and optimized for repeated developer use.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards and decorative glow.
- Monitoring surfaces that hide critical status behind hover, color, truncation, or horizontal scrolling.
- Spacious marketing layouts that sacrifice operational density or force excessive navigation.

## Design Principles

1. Optimize for triage speed: surface exceptions first and provide a direct path to their source.
2. Preserve context: keep agents, tasks, messages, dependencies, and activity connected.
3. Use progressive disclosure without hiding operational facts.
4. Keep dense information readable and structurally responsive from desktop to 360px.
5. Make keyboard, touch, and pointer interactions produce the same predictable result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Critical state and interaction must not depend on color or hover alone. The dashboard must preserve visible focus, reliable keyboard behavior, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and complete triage workflows at 360px and browser text zoom.
